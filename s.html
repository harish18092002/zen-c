Why do I need to select favorites?

  • Allows you to view your favorite images all together (even from other events)
  • Allows you to save changes you’ve made to images, such as cropping
  • Allows you to easily order multi-image products

How to create favorites:

  • « Previous Step
  • Next Step »

Step 1:

Select the image you wish to mark as a favorite

Step 2:

You can edit your image by Cropping, Zooming, Etc. If photographed on Green or Blue screen, you can select several different backgrounds and save those as new “favorite” images.

Step 3:

After editing your image, click the FAVORITE button under the enlarged image on the right side of the screen, OR click the heart icon under the preview image to save the original image as a favorite.

Step 4:

All images marked as FAVORITE will appear under the Favorites Tab Note: favorites from other event may also appear if previously marked. This allows you to order from numerous events and create multi-image products using images from other events.
